Hey Y'all Welcome to County As Hell, a Kentucky map game.

Do what now?

Every day, we'll give you 5 of Kentucky's 120 counties. You tell us where the hell they are.

Poke around the map and drop your pin. Think you've got it? Lock it in.

Come back tomorrow for 5 more. Lord knows we've got enough of 'em.
