Hey Y'all, welcome to County As Hell, a Kentucky map game.

Do what now?

What we're gonna do here is select the location of 5 different counties in Kentucky a day, then share it with your friends.

Navigate the map by dragging, pinching, and scrolling. Tap to drop a pin, and if you think you've nailed the county, well then lock it in.

Come back every day for a new set of 5 counties.
